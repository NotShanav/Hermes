Hermes v1.2.0 (desktop app 1.2.0 on Hermes core beta-0.1), 2026-11-09
Contains: Hermes core + desktop app (app/) + CI workflow.
Compiled by the author on GitHub Actions: NOT YET for this version (v1.1.0 compiled and ran; the changes below are new and untested).

New in v1.2.0 (Fluidity polish, Modern is untouched)
- Vivid backdrop: brighter glowing blobs (violet, magenta, cyan, blue), a coloured base gradient, soft floating glass orbs,
  fine film grain. Larger, livelier drift.
- Translucent glass cards with a light sheen and a bright gradient edge (cards ~70% opaque; text contrast kept).
- Stronger frosted chrome: more transparency, saturation and brightness behind sidebar, top bar, popovers, drawer, dialogs, toasts.
- Liquid glass v2: red/green/blue are refracted separately for colour fringing at the rim; stronger bend; a two-sided bright rim;
  a highlight that follows the cursor. Now also on the top bar, popovers, toasts and every primary button (plus the nav pill,
  segmented thumb, recording pill, range button and Unlock button from v1.1.0).
- Intensity: Full = all of the above; Subtle = frosted glass and two quiet blobs, no orbs, no refraction (lightest on the GPU).

Unchanged from v1.1.0: Modern/Fluidity setting, zoomable ribbon and lanes, layout hardening, fluid motion.
Earlier features still present: scroll/sidebar fix, USB-removal auto-lock, hidden clean-slate reset, author tag NotShanav.
