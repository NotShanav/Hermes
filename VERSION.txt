Compiled by the author on GitHub Actions: NOT YET for this version (v1.2.0 compiled and ran; the changes below are new and untested).

Fixes in v1.2.1 (Fluidity only; Modern is untouched)
- Glass rebuilt to follow the Liquid Glass spec: every glass surface keeps a transparent box and gets its frosted layer
  (translucent fill, blur 18-24px + saturate 165-180%, soft ambient shadow, inner top highlight) as a ::before pseudo-element,
  plus a 1px gradient refraction rim (::after). Cards, stat tiles, sidebar, top bar, popover, drawer, dialog, toast and the
  lock-screen panels are all translucent glass now (no solid backgrounds).
- Root cause of the grey / fringed glass in v1.2.0: lenses were nested inside elements that had their own backdrop blur,
  so they saw a half-transparent patch. Frost now lives on pseudo-elements, so lenses see the real background.
- Lenses sit behind text (they washed labels out in light mode) and bend a few pixels instead of ~20; removed from the top bar,
  popover and toast (they distorted the content inside). Kept on: nav pill, segmented thumb, recording pill, range button,
  primary buttons.
- Light mode: pastel mesh background (lavender, rose, sky), white translucent glass, darker rim hairline, quieter orbs.
- Unlock button: the lean effect measured the element it was moving (feedback loop, bouncy easing) so it slid away from the
  cursor. Now the outer element is fixed and only an inner element leans 3px, smoothly, springing back on release.
- Animations audited: glass surfaces animate transform only (opacity would break their blur); hover lift removed from buttons,
  chips and stat tiles (it made them flicker under the cursor), replaced by a glow; press = scale(0.97).
- Cosmetic: space above the notice in Settings > Security.

Unchanged: Modern/Fluidity setting, zoomable ribbon and lanes, layout hardening, USB-removal lock, hidden clean-slate reset.
